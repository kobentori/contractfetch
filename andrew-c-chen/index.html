If you keep the desk

The seat is $1,000 every four weeks, about $250 a week, month to month, cancel any time. What it replaces is a proposal consultant at $5,000 to $20,000 per bid, or a capture hire at $150,000 a year, for a firm that needs the reading done every week and the writing done a few times a year.

Every cycle the work shows up as the Fit-First Federal Feed, the weekly read of everything that posted in your lane with the false positives killed; the Bid-or-Bail Barometer, a bid, watch, or skip call with the reason on every item that survives; the Rival Recon Report, who won what in your lane and at what price, refreshed as awards post; the Compliance Cliff Calendar, every registration, certification, and vehicle date that can stop an award, watched; the Debrief Demand, a debrief request filed on every loss so the government tells us why; and the Teaming Table, two or three firms in your code holding work with your agencies, with the introduction drafted. When a bid clears your gate, the proposal draft gets written.

The first ten seats hold the founding rate for as long as they are held. After the ten, the seat is $1,500. The Top-Target Takedown Dossier, a full workup of the one contract you most want, comes with a founding seat. And I hold one firm per lane, which means once a competitor in your lane takes the seat, I cannot work yours. That is the only scarcity here, and it is real.

Radar-or-Refund

Every cycle you are a member, the work shows up: the feed, the calls, the watch, the drafts when a bid clears your gate. If a cycle ever comes back shallow, that cycle is free. I never promise you will win, and I never take a fee tied to an award. I promise the work.

Seats

Ten founding seats, one firm per lane. I am calling them the FY-Close Ten. The federal fiscal year ends September 30, which is when the last obligations of the year get written, and the founding cohort closes with it. After that the desk still opens, but not at the founding terms and not for ten. The nearest response date on your ladder is September 28, 2026, and the question window on a solicitation runs a week or two ahead of that, which is why this week matters more than next.
