Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Effective September 3, 2026

Contract Fetch is a federal capture desk run by its founder, Robert J. Coventry III. The service is built on public federal contracting records. This page says plainly what else we hold, where it came from, and how to ask us about any of it.

Where our research comes from

The contract intelligence we produce is drawn from public United States government sources: SAM.gov entity registrations and solicitations, USAspending.gov and the Federal Procurement Data System award records, agency forecasts, and published solicitation documents. A firm's registered name, address, UEI, NAICS codes, and award history are public records maintained by the government, and we use them as published.

For business outreach we may also hold the name, title, and work email address of a person at a federal contractor, obtained from that firm's own public registrations and website or from licensed business-contact providers. We use this information to write to firms about their own contracts. We do not buy or use consumer data, and we do not collect personal information about individuals outside their business role.

What the website records

When you open a briefing page addressed to your firm, the page records that it was opened, which briefing it was, and the referring page, so the founder knows a letter reached its reader. That record is tied to the firm, not to a tracking profile of you. The site does not run advertising trackers and does not set advertising cookies.

Booking a call goes through Calendly, and paying for a seat goes through Stripe. Each of those companies handles the details you enter under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, company, the time you booked, and, for paying members, the billing record Stripe passes back. We never see or store your card number.

Email

If you reply to a letter or an email from us, we keep the correspondence so the founder can act on it. If you write to say you do not want to hear from us, we stop, and we keep only what is needed to honor that request. Every outreach email carries a working reply address and a way to opt out.

Members

Members give us more: the details of their capability, past performance, and bid decisions, so the desk can do its work. That material is confidential to the engagement. We work with one firm per solicitation and never carry one member's information into work for another. We do not sell, rent, or trade any client or prospect information, ever.

Internal tools and Google accounts

Contract Fetch builds its own internal software. Some of that software connects to Google accounts that Contract Fetch or its founder owns, to read and send mail from those accounts. Where it does, our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That access is used only to operate the mailbox in question. Data from it is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as needed to run the service or as the law requires.

Keeping it, and your rights over it

We keep public-record research for as long as the service runs, because it is public and the record of a firm's contracts is the product. We keep correspondence and member material for the life of the engagement and a reasonable period afterward for our own records. You can ask what we hold about you or your firm, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete anything that is not a public government record. Write to the address below and the founder will answer personally.

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the effective date at the top changes with it. The current version is always at getcontractfetch.com/privacy.
