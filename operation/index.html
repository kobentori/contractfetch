The operation

Who does the work.

Every Capture Sprint that leaves this shop is produced the same way, by the same three layers. There are no handoffs and nothing is outsourced. This page is the whole operation - all of it.

Layer one

The founder.

Robert J. Coventry III reads every briefing before it ships and takes every call himself. He runs the federal capture desk at his family's firm, and he is a named inventor on two granted U.S. patents. The judgment calls - bid or no-bid, price, capture angle - are his, on every client, every time. They are never delegated, because judgment is the part a machine can't do.

Layer two

The engine.

The always-on layer. It sweeps every solicitation, award, and recompete in a client's lane across SAM, USAspending, and FPDS - every contract end-date clocked, every rival's wins logged, every set-aside and size gate checked against the client's actual registrations. Nothing it finds reaches a briefing until it survives the verify gate: re-confirmed against the primary government record, or thrown out.

It watches every day. It does not get sick, does not take vacation, does not miss a posting, and cannot be hired away by a competitor.

Layer three

The desk behind it.

Starside Security & Investigation - the family firm since 1998. A GSA schedule holder licensed across five states, running its own federal capture desk with nine figures on the table. That desk is where the method comes from: the line-by-line pricing rebuilds, the public-records pulls, the debrief demands, the wage-determination discipline. Contract Fetch is that desk's discipline, offered outside the family for the first time.

Why it's built this way

You aren't paying for bodies.

Most capture shops sell hours, so headcount is their product and your invoice grows with their payroll. Ours is built the other way: the engine does the watching, the founder does the judging, and the desk behind them keeps both honest. What you pay for is a lane that is watched every single day - and one person, reachable by phone, who is accountable for all of it.
